# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Locals and tourists in Djerba, Tunisia, navigating the public transport system. They need reliable, real-time bus information in a fast-paced or relaxed vacation context.

## Product Purpose
Community-powered bus detection. Validates the pulse of Djerba's transport by inferring moving buses from passive crowd GPS data. It transforms a standard utility into a sophisticated "insider" tool.

## Brand Personality
- **Apple/Tesla Minimalist**: Sleek, high-contrast, and hardware-precise.
- **Exclusive & Bold**: Evokes a sense of having insider access to the island's rhythm.
- **Luminous & Clean**: Pure light-mode aesthetic, rejecting the standard "tech" dark mode for a sun-drenched, premium feel.

## Anti-references
- Standard Material Design (floating action buttons, generic cards).
- Dark mode/Neon aesthetics.
- Cluttered, low-contrast maps.
- "Cheap" stock icons or overly colorful UI elements.

## Design Principles
- **Luminous Precision**: Use pure whites and subtle grays (tinted with the sky blue of Djerba) to create depth without clutter.
- **Bold Hierarchy**: Use high-contrast typography and generous whitespace to make the most important data (bus location, arrival time) unmistakable.
- **Hardware-Grade Interaction**: UI elements should feel physical and intentional, like premium automotive controls.
- **Insider Pulse**: Design for the "moment of discovery"—making the inference of a bus feel like a high-tech "catch" rather than a lucky guess.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- High contrast ratios (WCAG AA minimum) for outdoor visibility in Djerba's bright sunlight.
- Large tap targets for on-the-go usage.
- Simple, clear navigation for non-technical or multi-lingual users.
